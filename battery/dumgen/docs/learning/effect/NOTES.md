# Teaching notes

- Assume expert TypeScript knowledge; do not explain syntax, unions, generics, promises, or generators unless Effect changes their semantics.
- Assume only meme-level functional-programming vocabulary. Introduce FP names after the underlying operational idea is understood.
- Keep examples tied to Dumgen's actual Intake Batch, OpenAI adapter, resolution chain, and tf-demo orchestration.
- The learner is deciding whether Effect is worth its adoption cost, not seeking evangelism. Call out what Effect does not solve.
- Prefer short, direct lessons with one tangible win and a retrieval question at the end.
