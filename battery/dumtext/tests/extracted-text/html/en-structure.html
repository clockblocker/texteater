How to Brew Tea

You need three things:

  • Loose leaves, about 2 g per cup
  • Water just off the boil
    • 80 °C for green tea
    • 95 °C for black tea
    and a timer

  • A pot.

Then follow the steps:

  1. Warm the pot.
  2. Add the leaves.
  3. Pour and wait.
