# Dumrel Context

Dumrel names linguistic relations and defines the structural rules shared by
modules that produce, store, or consume relation graphs.

## Language

**Relation**:
A directed linguistic connection from one resolved entity to another resolved
entity of the same relation domain.

**Lexical Relation**:
A semantic connection between learner Readings, such as Synonym, Antonym, or
Hypernym.

**Morphological Relation**:
A structural connection between Lemmas, such as Derived From or Consists Of.

**Relation Family**:
The endpoint domain of a Relation: Lexical for Reading-to-Reading connections
or Morphological for Lemma-to-Lemma connections.

**Inverse Relation**:
The Relation that must point back from a target to its source; it may be the
same Relation, as with Synonym, or its paired counterpart, as with Hypernym and
Hyponym.
