# Classify multi-member Lexemes by whole-unit POS

A fixed multi-member Lexeme has one Kind for the whole lexical unit, even when
its members are discontinuous or have different standalone parts of speech.
German correlatives and fixed infinitival anchors therefore use their
whole-unit POS instead of the removed mixed `Construction/PairedFrame` model.
Canonical Form names the lexical identity; Attestation members preserve the
occurrence.

An article and its German common noun form one NOUN target. An article click
selects that noun; adjectives and non-article determiners remain separate.
Preposition/article Fusions keep Construction/Fusion classification.
