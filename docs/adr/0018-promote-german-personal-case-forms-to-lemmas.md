# Promote German personal case forms to Lemmas

Reviewed German personal-pronoun case forms are separate Lemmas, while Case,
grammatical Gender and Number, reflexivity, and historical status remain
Surface evidence. Formal `Sie`, `Ihnen`, and `Ihrer` also distinguish singular
and plural addressees through Core `referenceNumber`; an unknown addressee count
stays on the Open route. This favors stable learner navigation and authored
grammatical relations over a smaller inflectional Lemma inventory.
