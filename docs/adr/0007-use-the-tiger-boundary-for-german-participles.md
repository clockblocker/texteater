# Use the TIGER boundary for German participles

German classification follows the conservative TIGER participle boundary:
adjectivally used Partizip I and attributive or adverbial participles are
`Lexeme/ADJ`, while Partizip II in productive perfect and passive constructions
is `Lexeme/VERB`. For `sein + Partizip II`, a meaning-preserving active or
`werden`-passive counterpart keeps the occurrence verbal and places `sein` and
the participle in one click-invariant Analysis Target; lexicalized or
idiomatized property meanings remain a separate copula and `Lexeme/ADJ` target.
VERB Lemmas use the verbal Canonical Form, while actual ADJ Lemmas use the
participial adjective Canonical Form. This accepts contextual Lemma splitting
in exchange for preserving productive verbal identity and adjective grammar,
without introducing a separate Participle Kind.
