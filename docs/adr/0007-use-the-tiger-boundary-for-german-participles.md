# Use the TIGER boundary for German participles

German adjectival Partizip I and attributive or adverbial participles are
`Lexeme/ADJ`; productive perfect and passive Partizip II forms are
`Lexeme/VERB`. For `sein + Partizip II`, an active or `werden`-passive
paraphrase keeps the occurrence verbal, while a lexicalized property remains
adjectival. This preserves productive verbal identity without inventing a
Participle Kind.
